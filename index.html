Qurbani (Udhiya)

It is the Islamic religious practice of sacrificing an animal, such as a cow, goat, sheep, or camel, during Eid al-Adha to commemorate Prophet Ibrahim's (AS) obedience to Allah. The meat is traditionally shared among: The family. Relatives and friends. The poor and needy. It is also commonly called the Eid al-Adha Sacrifice Program or Qurbani Program.

Water Well Construction

In Islamic charitable organisations, it is commonly known as: Sadaqah Jariyah (Ongoing Charity) Program – providing clean water as a continuous act of charity. Water Well Donation Program – donors contribute money to construct wells. Community Borehole Project – providing clean water to villages and communities. Islamic Water Project – constructing wells in the name of Allah. The most appropriate Islamic name is: Sadaqah Jariyah Water Well Project. It provides lasting benefits because people and animals can continue accessing water long after the well is built.

Zakat Offering

Zakat Program is an Islamic charitable program that collects and distributes obligatory charity from eligible Muslims to people in need. Types of Zakat Programs Zakat al-Mal – Giving 2.5% of qualifying accumulated wealth. Zakat al-Fitr – Charity given before Eid al-Fitr. Zakat Distribution Program – Supporting poor and vulnerable people. Orphan Support Program – Helping orphaned children. Widow Support Program – Assisting widows. Education Support Program – Paying school fees for needy students. Medical Assistance Program – Helping people who cannot afford treatment. Food Distribution Program – Providing food to needy families. Other Islamic charity programs Qurbani (Animal Sacrifice) Program. Sadaqah Jariyah (Water Well Construction) Program. Ramadan Iftar Feeding Program. Mosque Construction Program. Islamic Education Program. Emergency Relief Program. These can all be organised under an Islamic Charity and Community Development Organisation.

Mosque Construction

Mosque Construction Program is an Islamic charitable initiative that builds new mosques, renovates existing mosques, and provides facilities for Muslims to worship Allah. It is also considered a form of Sadaqah Jariyah (Ongoing Charity). Types of Mosque Construction Programs New Mosque Construction Project – Building mosques in communities without one. Mosque Renovation Program – Repairing old or damaged mosques. Mosque Expansion Project – Increasing prayer space. Mosque Facilities Development – Constructing ablution areas, toilets and classrooms. Rural Mosque Construction – Building mosques in remote villages. Masjid Maintenance Program – Maintaining and improving mosque facilities. Islamic Centre Construction – Building centres for worship, education and community activities. Suggested Islamic Organisation Program Names Qurbani Program – Animal Sacrifice. Water Well Project – Clean Water Provision. Zakat Program – Obligatory Charity. Mosque Construction Program – Building Mosques. Ramadan Feeding Program – Feeding the Fasting. Orphan Sponsorship Program – Supporting Orphans. Sadaqah Program – Voluntary Charity. Islamic Education Program – Supporting Islamic Learning. Emergency Relief Program – Helping People in Crisis. All these can operate under an Islamic Charity and Humanitarian Development Program.
